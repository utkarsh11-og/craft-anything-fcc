XFCC app icon artwork

The app icon artwork in this directory is the XFCC replacement artwork created for this
project. It replaces the original Craft Anything raven icon and is not an ArtCraft asset.

The artwork is project-specific. The repository's MIT OR Apache-2.0 licence applies to the
surrounding project code and does not by itself establish a separate licence for this artwork.
Do not treat the original Craft Anything raven icon or ArtCraft brand assets as part of this
replacement artwork.
